Syrtis Major: Astronaut Survival & Science Guide

Mission Objective: Explore Early Noachian bedrock and collect hydrated minerals.

  1. Rule 1 (Terrain - MOLA)

    Walk only on flat basalt paths in the southeast. Strictly avoid the steep northwest caldera walls (1,800m drop).

  2. Rule 2 (Heat & Dust - THEMIS)

    Dark volcanic rocks absorb extreme heat. Keep suit cooling and dust filters active, and never walk across moving sand dunes.

  3. Rule 3 (Mining Targets - CRISM)

    Collect Iron & Titanium at the outer ridge, and Copper & Hydrated Silica at Nili Patera vents for base construction.

  4. Rule 4 (Oxygen & Signal Rule)

    Begin return to Base Camp before oxygen drops below 50%. Orbiter relay activates automatically if terrain blocks radio signal (Signal Delay: 14m 20s).
